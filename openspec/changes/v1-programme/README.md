# v1-programme

The full V1 programme: every capability from the PRD, as delta specs

## Strings for the clinical reviewer

The clinical lens of the 25 September 2026 review listed these as written for sign-off. The safeguarding editor added the last two as new strings. Every other user-facing string in the specs also goes to the reviewer before release.

- "What was going on just before?"
- "Mid-afternoon at 16:00 still happens."
- "Weekly swings of a kilo or two are normal and mean nothing on their own."
- "It passed" / "I ate as planned" / "I binged"
- "That deserves a person. Samaritans are there any time, on 116 123."
- "This is not a judgement about you."
- "This is not a diagnosis, and nothing here is closed to you."
- "Your record stays here, and you can keep adding to it."
- "Starred entries: 4 this week, 6 last week."
- "What did you notice this week?" / "What made things harder?" / "What helped?"
- "List every option, even the unlikely ones. Don't judge them yet."
- "Foods you keep away from"
- "Morning and evening reminders stop. The record stays. A check-in comes at 4, 8 and 12 weeks."
- "No person and no AI reads what you write. The app counts your starred entries, times and places to build your weekly review, on this device only."
- "\"Toast and tea\" is a complete entry." / "There is one star, \"felt like a binge\", and only you decide when it applies."
- The GP paragraph and its two variants.
- The alternatives examples.
- "Skipped, or not recorded yet?" and "Skipped, or was that 14:45?"
- "Urges rise, peak and fade. Most fade in about 20 minutes. Some take longer."
